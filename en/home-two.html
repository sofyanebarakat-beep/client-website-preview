FAQ

Quick answers to your questions

How does a custom project work?

After an initial conversation, we take on-site measurements, then produce a sketch and a detailed quote. Once approved, the piece is made in our workshop and installed by our team.

Which metals and finishes do you offer?

We work with wrought iron, steel and aluminium, with galvanising, powder-coating and a wide range of RAL colours for lasting protection against sea air.

What are the lead times?

Allow roughly 3 to 6 weeks between quote approval and installation, depending on the complexity of the piece and the chosen finish.

Is the quote free?

Yes. The visit, on-site measurements and the quote are free and with no obligation throughout the Nice area and the French Riviera.

Do you handle renovation and custom work?

Absolutely. We restore old gates, railings and grilles, and adapt every creation to the constraints of your home or building.
