Fences

Custom wrought iron fence: security, privacy and elegance

01
Study and measurements

Survey of the ground and property boundaries, choice of style, height and infill, then a detailed plan of the panels and posts.

02
Cutting and assembly

Bars, rails, flat bar and spear points are precisely cut, then welded in the workshop into ready-to-install panels.

03
Sanding and rust protection

Welds are ground and sanded, then each panel is stripped, degreased and protected with an anti-rust primer against the sea air.

04
Powder coating and installation

UV-resistant powder-coated finish (anthracite grey, matt or textured black, white), then installation on a low wall or base plates with precise alignment.

  • High impact resistance
  • Long service life
  • Customisable design
  • Enhanced security
  • Matches your gate
  • Installed on a low wall or in the ground

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to your questions

Clear answers to move forward with confidence on your ironwork project.

Free quote
What fence height should I choose?

Usually between 1.20 m and 2 m. We check your local planning rules with you before manufacturing.

Can a fence be installed on an existing low wall?

Yes. Panels are fixed on base plates or set into the wall, or installed in the ground on posts.

Can the fence match my gate?

Yes. Same design, same bars and same RAL colour: fence, gate and side gate form a harmonious whole.

Does an iron fence stand up to sea air?

Yes. Galvanising and powder coating give steel lasting protection against corrosion, even by the sea.

Is the quote free?

Yes. The visit, on-site measurements and the quote are free and with no obligation throughout the Nice area and the French Riviera.
