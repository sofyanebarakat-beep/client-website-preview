Gates

Custom wrought iron gate: elegance, security and craftsmanship

01
Study and measurements

Precise measurement of the entrance, choice of style and opening system, then a detailed plan: bars, decoration, hinges, lock and finishes.

02
Cutting and assembly

Square tubes, twisted bars, flat iron and forged elements are cut with precision, then welded in the workshop: frame, uprights, crossbars and ornaments.

03
Sanding and rust protection

Welds are ground and sanded, then the gate is stripped, degreased and protected with an anti-rust primer to withstand the weather.

04
Powder coating and installation

UV-resistant powder-coated finish (charcoal grey, matt or textured black, white), then installation on pillars with reinforced hinges and precise adjustments.

  • High impact resistance
  • Long service life
  • Customisable design
  • Enhanced security
  • Adds value to your property
  • Motorisation compatible

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to your questions

Clear answers to move forward with confidence on your ironwork project.

Free quote
How does a custom project work?

After an initial conversation, we take on-site measurements, then produce a sketch and a detailed quote. Once approved, the piece is made in our workshop and installed by our team.

Which metals and finishes do you offer?

We work with wrought iron, steel and aluminium, with galvanising, powder-coating and a wide range of RAL colours for lasting protection against sea air.

What are the lead times?

Allow roughly 3 to 6 weeks between quote approval and installation, depending on the complexity of the piece and the chosen finish.

Is the quote free?

Yes. The visit, on-site measurements and the quote are free and with no obligation throughout the Nice area and the French Riviera.

Do you handle renovation and custom work?

Absolutely. We restore old gates, railings and grilles, and adapt every creation to the constraints of your home or building.
